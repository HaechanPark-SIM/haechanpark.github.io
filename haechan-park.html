Research profile

Developing new materials and devices to bring electronics closer to people — and closer to circularity.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Chemistry at UNIST, working in Prof. Kyoseung Sim's research group. My research focuses on recyclable electronic materials, intrinsically soft devices, and functional systems for wearable and skin-interfaced applications.
