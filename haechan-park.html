Research profile

Exploring materials chemistry to advance soft electronic devices and their real-world applications.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Chemistry at UNIST, working in Prof. Kyoseung Sim's research group. My research focuses on the chemistry and design of soft electronic materials, intrinsically soft devices, and functional systems for wearable and skin-interfaced applications.
