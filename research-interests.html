Research Interests

Recyclable & Circular Electronics

Developing recyclable soft electronics through recoverable electrodes, organic semiconductors, and elastomers, with selective material separation and reuse for closed-loop device manufacturing.

All-soft & Stretchable Electronics

Engineering compliant materials and stretchable device architectures for soft transistors, pressure and temperature sensors, and functional electronic circuits that withstand repeated mechanical deformation.

Wearable & Skin-interfaced Systems

Integrating ultrathin, skin-conformal sensors into wearable platforms for monitoring physiological signals, pressure, and temperature, with applications in electronic skin and human–machine interfaces.
